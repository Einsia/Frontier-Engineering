# Wastewater Treatment

This domain collects control and optimization tasks for biological wastewater-treatment
plants. The benchmarks emphasize process dynamics, effluent compliance, energy use, and
robust operation under changing hydraulic loads.

## Tasks

- `BSM1AerationControl`
  - Unified benchmark: `task=unified task.benchmark=WastewaterTreatment/BSM1AerationControl`
  - Description: optimize zone aeration and internal recycle in an IWA BSM1-derived activated-sludge plant across dry, rain, and storm operation.
