FPGAPlacementOptimization UnifiedTask constraints:
1) Only modify `scripts/init.py`.
2) Preserve the output contract: the program must produce a `solution.pl`
   file in Bookshelf format with one line per instance: <name> <x> <y> <z>
3) Do not modify benchmark assets, documentation, references, verification
   code, runtime helpers, or `frontier_eval/` metadata.
4) Fixed instances (IO pads, PLLs) must remain at their original (x, y, z)
   coordinates from the input .pl file.
5) Every movable instance must be assigned to a legal (x, y, z) coordinate.
6) The placement must satisfy all three legality gates:
   - G1: Site-type compatibility (LUT/FF on SLICE, DSP on DSP, BRAM on BRAM)
   - G2: Resource capacity (max 16 LUT and 16 FF per SLICE site)
   - G3: Carry-chain integrity (adjacent vertical placement)
7) Prioritize correctness (legal placement) before optimization (HPWL).
