CacheReplacementPolicyOptimization constraints:
1) Only policy.py may be edited, and only inside its EVOLVE-BLOCK region.
2) The submission is a single online replacement policy: the victim chosen at
   access i may depend only on accesses 0..i of that run.
3) The workload (traces/kvcache_202206_traces_1.csv), the cache capacity, the
   evaluator (verification/), the reference model, the baseline and this file
   are frozen and must not be modified, re-read or regenerated.
4) The graded workload is a different real trace of the same family that the
   agent never receives: it is delivered to the submission only as the
   instance's key list at run time. Hard-coding keys or a trace cannot help.
5) The submission prints exactly one JSON object on stdout and exits 0
   (FE-BATCH-JSON-STDIO-V1). stdin is closed; no files other than argv[1] may
   be read; no network, no subprocesses, no wall-clock, no randomness.
6) The evaluator replays the submission under the frozen model: a hit may not
   evict, a miss on a full cache must evict exactly one resident key, and the
   submission is re-run on the first half of the same run, where it must make
   exactly the same decisions (an online policy does).
7) The hit rate may not exceed the offline optimum (Belady 1966); doing so is
   rejected, not rewarded.
8) Standard library only; deterministic; policy.py at most 64 KiB.
