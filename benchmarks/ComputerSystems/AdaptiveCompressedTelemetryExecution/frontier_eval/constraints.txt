   `// EVOLVE-BLOCK-END` in `scripts/init.cpp`; marker lines must remain intact.

   below the configured size bound, and each timed process has a 1 GiB address-space
   limit and a single pinned CPU.
6) Use self-contained C++20 and compiler-provided intrinsics only. No external
   libraries, downloaded assets, accelerators, or services are available.

   or `frontier_eval` metadata; these paths are checked read-only.
