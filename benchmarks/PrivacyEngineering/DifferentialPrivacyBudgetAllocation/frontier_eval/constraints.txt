5) Candidate timeout is 10 seconds per instance.
6) Candidate output is limited to 1048576 bytes.
7) Read-only benchmark assets include verification/, baseline/, reference/, data/, and references/.

   run in separate network-disabled, non-root containers.
9) Frontier unified must use its process isolation mode because the benchmark evaluator owns the
   inner candidate/verifier containers; do not wrap this evaluator in another Docker runtime.
