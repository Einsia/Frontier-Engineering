# Related Work

## Key References on Flexible Job Shop Scheduling

### Foundational Papers

1. **Bruker, P. & Schlie, R. (1990).** "Job-shop scheduling with multi-purpose machines." *Computing*, 45(4):369-375.
   - One of the earliest papers to introduce machine flexibility in job shop scheduling.

2. **Brandimarte, P. (1993).** "Routing and scheduling in a flexible job shop by tabu search." *Annals of Operations Research*, 41(3):157-183.
   - Introduced the widely-used Brandimarte FJSP benchmark instances.

3. **Hurink, J., Jurisch, B., & Thole, M. (1994).** "Tabu search for the job shop scheduling problem with multi-purpose machines." *OR Spektrum*, 15(4):205-215.
   - Extended FJSP with multi-purpose machines.

### Survey Papers

4. **Chaudhry, I.A. & Khan, A.A. (2016).** "A research survey: review of flexible job shop scheduling techniques." *International Transactions in Operational Research*, 23(3):551-591.
   - Comprehensive survey of FJSP solution methods.

5. **Amjad, M.K., et al. (2018).** "Recent research trends in genetic algorithm based flexible job shop scheduling problems." *Mathematical Problems in Engineering*, 2018:1-32.

### Worker Flexibility

6. **Li, J., Huang, Y., & Niu, X. (2014).** "A branch population genetic algorithm for dual-resource constrained job shop scheduling problem." *Computers & Industrial Engineering*, 68:113-125.
   - Considers both machine and worker resources in job shop scheduling.

7. **Lei, D. & Guo, X. (2015).** "An effective neighborhood search for scheduling in dual-resource constrained interval job shop with environmental objective." *International Journal of Production Research*, 53(19):5820-5833.

8. **Zambrano Rey, G., et al. (2014).** "Coupling a genetic algorithm with a discrete event simulation for solving the dual-resource constrained job shop scheduling problem." *International Journal of Production Research*, 52(19):5775-5795.

### Benchmark Instances

9. **Behnke, D. & Geiger, M.J. (2012).** "Test instances for the flexible job shop scheduling problem with work centers." *Working Paper, Helmut-Schmidt-University*.

10. **SAT Competition 2026 — Flexible Job Shop Scheduling Track.**
    See: https://satcompetition.github.io/2026/index.html
    - Standardized FJSP instances in SAT-encoded format.
